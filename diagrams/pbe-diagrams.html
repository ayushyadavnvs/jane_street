Programmable Bit Engine

Two small engines run short programs that drive, release and read pins. UART, SPI (controller and peripheral), I2C (controller, with SMBus PEC, and target), 1-Wire, Modbus RTU and WS2812 are programs loaded at runtime. Each engine also has a general CRC unit, a parity bit and a WAIT timeout, and sees its own window of 8 shared flags. This matches the RTL as built: 128-word program memory (each engine jumps within its own 64-word window from its START_PC), 4-byte FIFOs, about 29% of the 6×4 tile area.

4 · Engine registers

Each engine has 16 registers: engine 0 at 0x10–0x1F, engine 1 at 0x20–0x2F. All 16 slots except +7 are now in use.
