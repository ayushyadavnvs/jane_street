Jane Street protocol emulator ASIC · draft v0

Programmable Bit Engine

Two small engines run short programs that drive, release and read pins. UART, SPI and I2C are just different programs loaded at runtime. This matches the RTL as built: 64-word program memory, 4-byte FIFOs, about 15% of the 6×4 tile area.
